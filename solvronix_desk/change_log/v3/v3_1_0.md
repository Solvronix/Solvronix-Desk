## Version 3.1.0

### New

- **Grouped finance menus** — Accounts can now be split into Invoicing, Banking, Taxes,
  Budget and Accounts Setup, each with its own short menu. Switch it on in
  Theme Studio → Theme assignments & permissions. It is on for new installs; on an existing
  site it stays off until you choose it. Switching it off puts everything back.
- **Activity panel on forms** — on wide screens a record's comments and timeline sit in the
  right-hand column, with a button in the form toolbar to hide them.
- **Stat buttons** — a customer's invoices, an invoice's payments and other linked documents
  show as small buttons with counts at the top of the form.
- **Dashboard date filter** — one date range at the top of a workspace changes every chart and
  count on it; choose "All time" to go back.
- **Save & New in pop-ups** — the small pop-ups for contacts, customers, items and more have a
  Save & New button for entering several records in a row.
- **Sales | Purchase tabs** — one click to flip between the sales and purchase invoice lists.
