## Version 3.0.0 — Workspace Classic

### New

- **Workspace Classic** — a new desk layout with a top menu bar instead of the sidebar.
  The bar shows the current module's menus; on phones and tablets they open from ☰.
- **App home screen** — open it from the apps button (⋮⋮⋮) or **Alt+H**, type to search,
  and use the arrow keys to open an app.
- **Forms as a sheet** — forms sit on a white sheet with status steps (workflow, or
  Draft → Submitted/Paid) and a corner ribbon for paid, cancelled and disabled records.
- **Dark mode** — Workspace Classic now looks right in dark mode: dark pages, tables and
  buttons with readable text. Themes with light custom colours get the same fix.

### Try it

- Use the buttons just below these notes, or the small **"New in Solvronix Desk 3.0"** card
  that everyone who may choose a theme sees once.
  **Try it now** switches only your own Desk; a bar then lets you **Keep it** or
  **Switch back**. You can change it later in **All Options → Appearance → Theme profile**.
- System Managers can click **Make it the default for everyone**: Theme Studio opens with
  Workspace Classic loaded as a draft. Nothing changes for others until you **Publish theme**.
- Don't want the card? Theme Studio → **Theme assignments & permissions** → untick
  **Show feature announcements**.

Your current layout stays as it is until someone switches. No Frappe version is dropped.
